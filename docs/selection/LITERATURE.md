## Gaps the literature does not close

* No published work that the search found compares complete QEM protocols (calibration, training and target shots
  charged against one total budget) across many circuits and budgets and learns when each wins; the closest are
  method-specific overhead analyses (Temme et al.; van den Berg et al.; Cai et al.) and decision-focused critiques
  (Scavino 2026). The study's claims are therefore its own, with the uncertainty stated in each figure.
* Conformal and selective-prediction guarantees assume exchangeability of the units; families, devices and widths
  outside the training set break it, which is why 1.7.0 reports those splits as measured generalization only.
