# QEM Explorer 1.7.0 — response to the independent review of 1.6.0

Every finding of the review (`QEM_Explorer_1.6.0_Independent_Review.md`, 20 October 2026) was treated as a hypothesis
and checked against the 1.6.0 source, its saved data and the review's evidence scripts before anything was changed.
This document is the issue-to-evidence matrix: for each item its status, the evidence, what it affects, the
correction and its priority, the regression test or validation experiment, and what had to happen to existing data.

Status: **confirmed**, **partially confirmed**, **not reproduced**, **rejected** (with a counterexample or test).
Priority: **P0** foundational correctness (fixed before any new data was collected), **P1** evaluation and claims,
**P2** usability and presentation. Data action: **relabel** (new trials), **reanalyze** (same trials, corrected
analysis), **replot** (same tables), **none**.

Tests named below are in `tests/`; `test_selection_v170.py` collects the selection invariants.

## 6. Findings beyond the review

* N1 — CDR on a Clifford or degenerate trial design returned its fallback without a flag (S4 extended).
* N2 — `design.expand` skipped variants whose construction raised a `ValueError` (an invalid configuration looked
  valid) (S6 extended).
* N3 — CDR's training-set search seeded its stages with `seed + 101 + 7919·i`, the same lattice as the 1.6.0 design
  seeds `seed + 7919·k`: design k's first stage could coincide with design 0's stage k. Fresh pools use
  SeedSequence-derived seeds (no shared lattice).
* N4 — `run.profile` used `resource` (POSIX only).
* N5 — The mismatch study's ten circuits are pilot circuits (2 of them in the 1.6.0 final test); its model was trained
  without them (D22), so this is recorded, not a leak.
* N6 — For deployment, CDR and NIL are infeasible on Clifford circuits and on allocation designs with degenerate
  training labels (the study's allocation rule); the structural feasibility check reproduces the study on every
  pilot row except degenerate-training cases, which need the training circuits' ideal values (1.4 % of rows; tested).
* N7 — The hardware plan charged a merged PEC circuit its shots once per occurrence (≈ 3·10⁸ shots for a run at
  B = 20 000), declaring the shipped plan infeasible; every run is now charged exactly B
  (`test_hardware_plan_charges_each_protocol_run_exactly_its_budget`).
* N8 — Binding: device digests of runs bound before the `g1`/`g2`/`t` device modifiers existed differ from the new
  ones only by those keys; such runs resume (equivalence under the earlier key set, tested), other changes are
  still refused. The coherent-fraction devices needed the exact engine in their digest (fixed, tested).
* N9 — Figures, found by viewing every drawn figure: Figure 1's legend collapsed the layout, Figure 3's labels and
  noise-composition cells overlapped, legends covered data in Figures 3, 5, 6 and two supplementary figures, the
  design-study panel's rows were unlabelled and unordered, the coverage figure omitted the confirmation set, and
  methods boxes reported the mismatch study's r for the development set. Presentation only; the validator checks the
  plotted numbers.
