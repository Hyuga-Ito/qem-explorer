**Theme.** An evidence-based repair of the 1.4.2 QA findings, followed by a research upgrade.

**Highlights.**

- Corrected protocol moments for CDR, FR and NIL. They are validated against 6 000 independent experiments per audit case.
- Correct readout γ.
- Bounded image export.
- Budget preflight.
- Atomic guides that replace or merge contenders.
- Explicit input and derived output.
- Cited presets.
- One experiment schema for the UI, YAML and the CLI.
- Validated discrepancy measures with multiple testing.
- The main-study framework: features, designs, a confirmatory split, regime maps and a recommender with abstention.
- A campaign ledger for QPU usage.
- Two research-area pages.

**Not in this release.**

- No hardware results.
- No license yet: an owner decision is required.
